Privacy Policy

How The Original Patch Master handles information you send through this website.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Who we are

The Original Patch Master is a drywall and plaster company based at 25244 Annapolis St, Dearborn Heights, MI. This policy covers this website, originalpatchmaster.com.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through our forms:

  • Estimate and contact requests: your name, phone number, email address, city, the service you need, your message, and your preferred contact method.
  • Job applications: your name, phone, email, location, work experience, availability and any description you write.
  • Feedback: a star rating and any comments, name or contact details you choose to add.
  • Text message sign-up: if you use our SMS opt-in page, your name, mobile number, optional email address and your text message choices.
  • Text message choices: whether you agreed to receive text messages, the wording you saw, when you agreed, and which page you used.
  • Form details: the page a form was sent from and the time it was sent.

This website does not currently add analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, prepare estimates and schedule appointments.
  • To send service-related communication about your inquiry, estimate, appointment or job.
  • To consider job applications.
  • To keep a record of your requests and your text message choices.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages (SMS)

We send text messages only if you agree by checking the optional text message box on a form, or by otherwise agreeing to receive them. Agreeing is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase or of requesting an estimate or any service. You must be 18 or older to sign up for text messages from The Original Patch Master. You can also sign up on our SMS opt-in page.

  • Service-related messages cover your inquiries, estimates, appointments and service updates. If you separately opt in to marketing texts, you may also receive occasional promotional messages, such as seasonal offers and review requests. Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at 734-489-2284 or raegirlg@icloud.com.
  • The Original Patch Master does not sell or share text message consent or mobile phone numbers with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

See our Terms & Conditions for the full text message terms.

Who handles your information

We use service providers to run our business operations: Formspree receives and delivers the forms you submit on this website, and GoHighLevel manages customer records, appointments and messaging. These providers process your information for us. We may also share information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and security

We keep information for as long as we need it to respond to you, provide estimates and services, keep business records, or meet legal requirements. We rely on our service providers' security measures. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To ask about, correct or delete the information you sent us, contact us at 734-489-2284 or raegirlg@icloud.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
