Plaster Repair

About Plaster Repair

Plaster walls and ceilings do not behave like drywall. They crack differently, and a repair has to bond to what is already there. The Original Patch Master repairs plaster and finishes it smooth.

The first step is checking how sound the plaster around the damage is, because that decides what the repair needs.

What It Involves

What the Work Includes

  • Filling and repairing cracks
  • Patching holes and damaged areas
  • Repairing loose or crumbling sections
  • Smoothing the repair into the surrounding surface
  • Dustless sanding with specialized equipment
  • Leaving the surface ready for paint

Common Situations

When people call us for plaster

  • Cracks that keep coming back

    Cracks in walls and ceilings, from thin lines to wide splits.

  • Damage from other work

    Openings left by plumbing, wiring or fixtures that were moved.

  • Loose or crumbling plaster

    Sections that sound hollow or are coming away from the wall.

  • Plaster next to drywall

    One wall plaster, the next drywall. We repair both.

Our Approach

How we handle plaster

We check the area around the damage before we quote the job. If the surrounding plaster is solid, a patch is enough. If it is loose, a bigger section may need work, and we tell you that up front.

The estimate is free and we explain what we found. Repairs are finished smooth, and sanding is done with specialized dustless equipment.

Plaster Repair: Common Questions

Can you repair plaster and drywall in the same room?

Yes. The Original Patch Master does both, so one estimate covers the room.

What if the plaster is cracked in several places?

We look at the whole wall or ceiling during the estimate and tell you what it needs. Several cracks do not automatically mean a bigger job.

Is plaster repair messy?

Sanding makes dust on any plaster or drywall job. We use specialized equipment to cut it down.
