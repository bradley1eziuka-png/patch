Basements, Garages & Houses

Basements, Garages & Houses

Finishing a basement, a garage or a whole house means a lot of drywall, a lot of finishing and a schedule that has to hold together. This is a priority for The Original Patch Master, and we take projects at that scale.

We hang, tape, finish and sand, with insulation available while the walls are open.

Three Kinds of Projects

Basements

A basement can add a lot of living space. We hang and finish the drywall so the space is ready for paint, and we can handle insulation before the walls close.

Garages

Finished walls and ceilings turn a garage into a cleaner, more usable space. We hang the drywall, finish it and handle insulation if the garage needs it.

Whole houses and new construction

Whole-house jobs mean drywall in every room. Every room gets the same process: hang, tape, finish and sand.

What It Involves

What the Work Includes

  • Hanging drywall on walls and ceilings
  • Taping and finishing seams and corners
  • Insulation before the walls close up
  • Sanding with dustless equipment
  • Level 5 finishing where you want it
  • One free estimate for the whole project

Common Situations

Projects we take on

  • A basement you want to use

    Open framing or bare block, ready for walls and ceilings.

  • A garage with unfinished walls

    Anything from basic drywall to a smoother finish.

  • A new build or addition

    Drywall for every room, start to finish.

  • A house after major work

    Extensive demolition or a remodel where most rooms need new drywall.

Our Approach

How a large job starts

Big jobs start with a conversation. Tell us what the space is, how you want it to look and when you want it done. We give you a free estimate, explain the scope and answer your questions.

Our part is the drywall, insulation and finishing. If your project involves other trades, mention that when you call so we can talk about how the work fits together.
