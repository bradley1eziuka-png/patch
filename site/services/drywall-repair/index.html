Drywall Repair

About Drywall Repair

Drywall gets damaged in every house. A doorknob goes through a wall, a plumber cuts an opening, a seam cracks along the ceiling. The Original Patch Master repairs new and old drywall and finishes the area so you do not see where the damage was.

One small hole is a normal job for us. So is a room with damage in several places.

What It Involves

What the Work Includes

  • Patching holes and dents
  • Fixing cracked seams and corners
  • Cutting out and replacing damaged sections
  • Taping, mudding and sanding the repair
  • Blending the repair into the wall or ceiling around it
  • Dustless sanding with specialized equipment

Common Situations

When people call us for repairs

  • Doorknob and furniture damage

    Holes of any size, from a dent in a hallway to a gap where a door swung into the wall.

  • Openings left by other trades

    Plumbers, electricians and HVAC installers cut into drywall. Someone has to close it back up.

  • Damage after a leak

    Once the leak is fixed, wet or stained drywall can be cut out and replaced.

  • Cracks and popped seams

    Cracks along seams and corners, and above doors and windows.

Our Approach

How we handle a repair

We look at the damage first. Some repairs need a patch. Others need a larger section replaced. We tell you which, and you get a free estimate before we start.

The repair is hung, taped and finished in stages, then sanded smooth. Our specialized dustless sanding equipment cuts down on the dust that ends up in your rooms.

Drywall Repair: Common Questions

Do you take small repair jobs?

Yes. A single hole is a normal job for us. The Original Patch Master also handles larger repairs and full drywall projects.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Call 734-489-2284 or describe the damage on the contact form.

Will I be able to see the repair?

The goal is a repair you cannot see once the wall is painted. Tell us about the wall when you call and we will explain what to expect.
