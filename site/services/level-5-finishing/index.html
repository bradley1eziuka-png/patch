Level 5 Finishing

About Level 5 Finishing

Drywall finishing is graded in levels, and Level 5 is the highest. After the seams are taped and finished, a thin skim coat of joint compound goes over the entire surface. The result is an even wall, with no visible difference between the taped joints and the rest of the board.

The Original Patch Master offers Level 5 finishing as part of drywall installation and finishing jobs.

What It Involves

What the Work Includes

  • Taping and finishing all seams and corners
  • A skim coat across the whole surface
  • Sanding smooth with dustless equipment
  • A surface ready for primer and paint

Common Situations

Where Level 5 makes sense

  • Walls with strong side light

    Windows, skylights and hallway lights show every ridge and seam.

  • Glossy or satin paint

    Higher-sheen paint makes flaws easier to see.

  • Smooth walls with no texture

    Nothing on the wall hides an imperfection.

  • Ceilings

    Light hits ceilings at an angle, and seams show more than most people expect.

Our Approach

How we decide

Level 5 is not needed in every room. A closet or a garage may not call for it. We look at how light hits the wall and what paint you plan to use, then tell you whether Level 4 or Level 5 makes sense.

Level 5 takes more time than a standard finish, and the estimate reflects that. The estimate is free and we explain the number.

Level 5 Finishing: Common Questions

What is the difference between Level 4 and Level 5?

Level 4 has taped and finished seams. Level 5 adds a skim coat over the entire surface, which evens out the difference between the seams and the rest of the board.

Do I need Level 5 in every room?

No. Rooms with strong light, glossy paint or smooth ceilings benefit most. We will tell you if a standard finish is enough.

Does Level 5 make more dust?

Any sanding makes dust. We use specialized dustless sanding equipment on our work.
