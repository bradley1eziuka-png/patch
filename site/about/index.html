Who We Are

Skilled Craftsmen. Quality Finishes.

At The Original Patch Master, our team of highly qualified craftsmen takes pride in delivering professional drywall, plaster repair, and finishing work. From commercial projects to detailed repairs, we focus on quality workmanship, reliability, and results that speak for themselves.

With 25 years in the trade, we handle commercial drywall installation, plaster repair and finishing, as well as residential repairs and larger projects like basements and garages.

The Original Patch Master is a family-owned company based in Dearborn Heights. Every job starts with a free estimate and a straight explanation of what the work involves.

Commercial Work

Commercial Drywall & Plaster

Commercial drywall installation, repair and finishing is a priority for The Original Patch Master. We hang, tape, finish and sand, and we repair plaster, in commercial properties across Metro Detroit.
